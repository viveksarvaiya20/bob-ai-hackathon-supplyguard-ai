# Problem Statement

## Context

Modern logistics operations depend on a fleet of vehicles, time-sensitive shipments, and routes that can be affected by traffic, weather, vehicle issues, and blocked roads.

## Problem

Managers need to answer several questions quickly:

1. Which shipments are most likely to be delayed?
2. Which vehicles are overloaded or underutilized?
3. Which disruptions are important enough to act on now?
4. Can a risky shipment be moved to another vehicle safely?

Manual analysis across these factors is slow and can lead to missed delivery risks and poor fleet utilization.

## Target Users

- Logistics managers
- Fleet managers
- Dispatchers
- Supply-chain operations teams

## Goal

Provide a single decision-support dashboard that turns fleet and disruption information into understandable risk signals and actionable vehicle reassignment recommendations.
